1. What roles do the terminal, shell and kernel play?
2. How do you check your location before changing a file?
3. What differs between cp and mv, and between |, > and >>?

a partner and ask them to reproduce one count. If alone, rerun that count yourself

??? success "Recall anchors"
    The terminal displays text; Bash interprets commands; programs request kernel services. pwd anchors relative paths. cp preserves its source; mv relocates it. A pipe connects programs; > replaces a file; >> appends. Log records alone do not establish patient counts, clinical impact or root cause.
